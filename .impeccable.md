## Design Context

### Users
A single person who has tried Actual Budget and other trackers that shut down, got worse, or felt like work. They sit down to categorize spending and check whether this month is on track, usually at a desk, sometimes in a narrow window. They want control (edit, split, delete, bulk) without envelope jargon.

### Brand Personality
Sparse, fast, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a well-set ledger, not a startup dashboard and not a spreadsheet. Calm confidence; numbers do the talking.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark editorial ledger: warm near-black, cream ink, one gold accent, Palatino-like serif. Anti-references: Actual Budget's crowded navigation, YNAB envelope chrome, Inter-on-white SaaS, icon-per-heading templates. Light mode is not required in v1.

### Design Principles
1. Three working screens: Month, Activity, Categories & Rules. Setup sits behind a cog and stays out of the way.
2. Keyboard is the fast path for categorization (type a name, Enter).
3. Uncategorized is the default action list.
4. Never show bank credentials, Access URLs, or setup tokens in logs or flash copy.
5. Empty, error, and syncing states are first-class, not afterthoughts.
